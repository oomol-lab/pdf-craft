# PDF Craft v2.6.7

Restore incremental PDF extraction `on_body` / `on_footnote` delivery after the
2.6.6 regression. Closed page-processed paragraphs and footnotes arrive while OCR and
later analysis are still running, without a duplicate replay at completion.

Page refinement now runs as a bounded pipeline: JEV starts when its initial
neighbor inputs are ready; the target LLM follows only that page's score. Neighbor
scores and refined neighbor content never enter its prompt. Ordered assembly
emits closed processed paragraphs/footnotes without waiting for whole-book OCR,
other JEV jobs or final TOC. This supersedes the temporary pre-refinement preview.
The initial paragraph boundary survives intervening image/table-only pages without
retaining those pages in memory. Cancellation also interrupts a full refinement
window, then settles its page jobs before releasing providers and workers.
OCR completions now enter refinement by neighborhood readiness rather than a
contiguous page prefix. An unrelated earlier slow OCR page no longer blocks a
ready JEV/LLM job. Cross-window identities remain resolved at ordered assembly;
finished later jobs free request slots while their results wait on disk.

Callbacks describe page-processed content; final TOC/heading levels and directory
exclusion may organize the PCEX differently. Final TOC, chapter and furniture consistency checks remain in place. See [streaming callbacks](../en/STREAMING.md)
and [中文说明](../zh-CN/STREAMING.md).

Ordered delivery, source identities, disk buffering, callback errors and cancellation
remain supported. Barrier regressions guard timely delivery independently of unrelated pages,
whole-book scoring and final packaging. No progress event or PCEX format change.
