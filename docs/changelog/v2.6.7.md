# PDF Craft v2.6.7

Restore incremental PDF extraction `on_body` / `on_footnote` delivery after the
2.6.6 regression. Closed primary paragraphs and footnotes arrive while OCR and
later analysis are still running, without a duplicate replay at completion.

These callbacks expose primary extraction results: content, ownership and heading
levels may differ from the final refined PCEX. Final chapter adoption, TOC and
furniture validation remain unchanged. See [streaming callbacks](../en/STREAMING.md)
and [中文说明](../zh-CN/STREAMING.md).

Ordered delivery, source identities, disk buffering, callback errors and cancellation
remain supported. Barrier regressions guard timely delivery independently of final
refinement and packaging. No progress event or PCEX format change.

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/oomol-lab/pdf-craft/compare/v2.6.6...v2.6.7
